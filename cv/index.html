About

I am a software architect and developer based in Hamburg. For fifteen years I have built systems across the full stack — .NET services and APIs, the frontends in front of them, and the Azure, Kubernetes and CI/CD infrastructure underneath — for energy trading, logistics, public administration and payment technology.

New technology is what keeps the work interesting. I read up on where the field is moving and try things out early, and I care as much about how software gets built as about what gets built: architecture, tests and the delivery pipeline are part of the product rather than overhead around it.
